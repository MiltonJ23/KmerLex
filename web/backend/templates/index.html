About Kmerlex

Kmerlex is a genomic k-mer analysis platform developed at the University of Yaoundé for African genomics research.

🔬 Research Focus:
  • Malaria genomics (Plasmodium falciparum)
  • HIV genetic diversity
  • African population genetics
  • Infectious disease surveillance
